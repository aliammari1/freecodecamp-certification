# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming to newcomers — this repo is intentionally beginner-friendly.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the learner reading the code.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, trolling, publishing
others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainer at **ammari.ali.0001@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
